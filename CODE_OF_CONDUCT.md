# Community guidelines

AmberGate welcomes people with different experience levels. Questions, bug reports and contributions in English or Russian are welcome.

- Discuss the idea or implementation; keep criticism specific and constructive.
- Be patient with newcomers and provide enough context for others to help.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or impersonate others.
- Do not publish another person's private information or credentials.
- Avoid spam, unsolicited promotion and unrelated content.

These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions. Maintainers may remove disruptive content, lock conversations or limit participation when necessary. For abusive content, use GitHub's **Report content** control; do not repeat private information publicly. Technical vulnerabilities belong in the [private security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).

## Правила общения

Вопросы и вклад на русском и английском приветствуются. Обсуждайте решения уважительно, помогайте новичкам и давайте контекст. Оскорбления, угрозы, дискриминация, публикация чужих данных и спам недопустимы. Для жалобы на нарушение используйте **Report content** в GitHub. Уязвимости направляйте через [приватный канал](SECURITY.md).
